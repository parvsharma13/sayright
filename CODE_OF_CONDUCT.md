# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent to people. Assume good faith. Critique code, not the person who wrote it.

## What we expect

- **Focus on the work.** Disagree about a design, a trade-off or a diff as much as you
  like. Do not make it about the person holding the position.
- **Assume competence and good intent.** Contributors here have different amounts of
  Swift, macOS and Accessibility API experience. A question that seems obvious to you is
  not obvious to everyone, and answering it plainly costs nothing.
- **Accept that "no" is a legitimate answer.** This project deliberately stays small.
  A declined feature is not a judgement of you, and the reasons are written down in
  [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md#what-is-likely-to-be-declined).
- **Be clear about uncertainty.** "I tested this in TextEdit but not in Word" is more
  useful than implied confidence.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, intimidation, or sustained disruption of discussion.
- Personal attacks, demeaning remarks, or insults — including ones framed as jokes.
- Discriminatory language or behaviour relating to age, disability, ethnicity, gender
  identity or expression, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual
  orientation.
- Sexual language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention of any kind.
- Publishing anyone's private information without their explicit permission.
- Deliberately introducing malicious code, or using the project's Accessibility access as
  an attack vector.

This applies to issues, pull requests, commit messages, code comments, discussions, and
anywhere else people interact on behalf of the project.

## Enforcement

For abusive content on GitHub, use the content's **Report content** option. To request
maintainer help, open an issue asking for a private reporting channel without including
personal information or sensitive details. No public contact email is provided.

Maintainers may edit or delete comments, close or lock threads, and block accounts.
For serious or repeated behaviour, that means a permanent ban. Maintainers are held to
this document too — more so, not less.

If you believe enforcement was unfair, say so in the same channel and it will be looked
at again.

---

This policy applies to all contributors and maintainers.
